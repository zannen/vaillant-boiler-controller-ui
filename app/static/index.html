Heating schedule

Sent to the boiler with each instruction while the schedule is enabled or an override is on; otherwise the boiler's own knobs apply. The boiler's d.71 setting (75 °C unless changed) still caps the heating flow.
Heating runs only while the boiler's own timer is also on; the schedule can switch it off, not on. Times are 24-hour HH:MM: 00:00 is the start of the day and 24:00 the end.
